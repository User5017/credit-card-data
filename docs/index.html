Data and method

  • Download every series as one CSV (metric, entity, tier, period_end, period_type, value, source, pulled_at).
  • Every period is dated to its last day: month end, quarter end, or the Wednesday for weekly bank data. A quarterly survey taken in May is shown at June 30; the July Senior Loan Officer survey, which asks about the quarter just ended, is shown at June 30.
  • Step charts hold each reading across its own period. Nothing is interpolated between readings. Charts open on the last 5 years; the button on each card shows the full history.
  • Shaded bands are NBER recessions (peak month to trough month). Dashed grey lines are the 2015-2019 average of the first series on the chart, the last full expansion before the pandemic.
  • SA means seasonally adjusted. Series are labeled SA or NSA and are never compared across that line.
  • Different sources measure different populations. G.19 covers all lenders; the H.8, charge-off and APR series cover commercial banks only; the Y-14 series cover the largest banks; the NY Fed series are credit reports, all lenders; the FDIC series are bank charters. Notes under each chart say which, with a link to the source.
  • "Data as of" on a chart is when its newest value was loaded. A card carries a status badge when its source is not OK; "last fetch attempt" is then the run that failed. "Checked against the release" lists the numbers the pipeline reproduces from the source's own release page on every run.
  • The latest readings are computed from the series on the page: latest value, change against the same period a year earlier, the 2015-2019 average for rates, and whether the value is the highest or lowest in at least three years. No model writes any of it.
  • Sources: Federal Reserve Board, via FRED; CFPB Terms of Credit Card Plans survey; Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia, Large Bank Credit Card and Mortgage Data (FR Y-14M); Federal Reserve Bank of New York, Quarterly Report on Household Debt and Credit (Consumer Credit Panel/Equifax); FDIC, Call Report data via the BankFind Suite API; Federal Reserve Bank of New York, Survey of Consumer Expectations Credit Access Survey. The Federal Reserve Board series (G.19 Consumer Credit, H.8 Assets and Liabilities of Commercial Banks, Charge-Off and Delinquency Rates, Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey) are retrieved from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
