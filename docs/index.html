Data and method

  • Download every series as one CSV (metric, entity, tier, period_end, period_type, value, source, pulled_at).
  • Every period is dated to its last day: month end, quarter end, or the Wednesday for weekly bank data. A quarterly survey taken in May is shown at June 30.
  • Step charts hold each reading across its own period. Nothing is interpolated between readings.
  • SA means seasonally adjusted. Series are labeled SA or NSA and are never compared across that line.
  • Different sources measure different populations. G.19 covers all lenders; the H.8, charge-off and APR series cover commercial banks only; the CFPB card-terms survey describes terms offered per card product, not rates actually paid. Notes under each chart say which.
  • Sources: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System (G.19 Consumer Credit, H.8 Assets and Liabilities of Commercial Banks, Charge-Off and Delinquency Rates, Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey), retrieved from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
