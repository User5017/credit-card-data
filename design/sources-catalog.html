How they join

Time is the spine; everything joins on month or quarter. Four other dimensions need a one-time crosswalk you build and maintain — that crosswalk is the actual asset.

Issuer

  • RSSD_ID (call reports, Y-9C)
  • CIK (10-Q, 8-K, XBRL)
  • Trust name (10-D)
  • Institution name (TCCP)
  • Company name (complaints)

~30 issuers. Build once, touch on M&A (Discover → Capital One).

Credit tier

  • CFPB: superprime 720+ · prime 660–719 · near-prime 620–659 · subprime 580–619 · deep subprime <580
  • Philly Fed: FICO buckets
  • TCCP: issuer-declared target tiers
  • TU / Equifax: VantageScore bands
  • SLOOS / SCE: prime vs subprime, self-reported score

Merchant category

  • BEA PCE line
  • Census NAICS
  • Visa/MC merchant category (MCC groups)
  • Atlanta Fed merchant type
  • CFPB 2025 report categories
  • BofA categories

Approximate by nature — Amazon, Walmart, Costco smear across lines. State the tolerance and move on.

Geography / cohort

  • State: NY Fed, complaints
  • Age: NY Fed, BLS, SCE
  • Income: BLS, SCE, SHED

What is not public

Net interchange for the big three banks

JPM, Citi, BofA report card income already net of rewards. You can bound it, not observe it. Amex, Capital One, Synchrony, Bread are the ones that show their work.

Rewards cost by credit tier

Only in the CFPB biennial. Between editions you interpolate from issuer mix.

Realized APR by tier

Philly Fed (score buckets) and the biennial. TCCP gives offered ranges, not what's actually paid.

Anything account-level

Y-14M raw data is regulatory-only. Philly Fed is the closest public shadow.

Card spend by merchant category, monthly

Only BofA's PDF, and only their customers. Total spend by category (BEA/Census) is the proxy, bridged through Atlanta Fed card-share.

Pricing by state

No public source. Balances and complaints by state, yes; APR by state, no.

Dashboard v1 — grade-A only, monthly refresh

Nine feeds, all scripted, zero PDFs. Enough to run the growth / pricing / performance story every month before touching a parser.

Growth panel

  • G.19 revolving, by holder
  • NY Fed balances by age & state
  • Call reports: top-15 card banks + 8 sponsor banks, card loans QoQ

Pricing panel

  • G.19 APR, both series
  • TCCP: median APR by issuer size × tier, and the large–small spread over time
  • Philly Fed: utilization, revolve share, by score bucket

Performance panel

  • Fed charge-off series, top-100 vs rest
  • Call-report NCO per bank
  • SLOOS standards + SCE rejection rates

Spend panel

  • BEA PCE top-30 lines, 12-mo growth
  • Census retail by NAICS
v2 adds the two B-grade feeds that carry the most signal: the four issuers' monthly 8-K metrics (a 6-week lead on the quarterly data) and the 10-Q economics lines for AXP / COF / SYF / BFC (yield, interchange, rewards, and the give-back ratio). v3 is the PDF layer — trusts, network schedules, the biennial — where the parsers earn their keep.
